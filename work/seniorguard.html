SeniorGuard

An IoT health-monitoring system that watches over elderly residents and automatically alerts caregivers in an emergency.

Role
Hardware, firmware & cloud
Timeline
November 2024
Scale
Sensor network across 10+ households
Reliability
99%+ uptime

The problem

Someone who lives alone can be fine, until suddenly they aren't.

Families and caregivers want to know an older person is okay without constant calls or intrusive cameras. And the system has to work when it counts, without asking the user to learn a new device.

What I built

  • A sensor network deployed across 10+ households, built in C/C++ on embedded hardware.
  • Real-time health monitoring with automated emergency alerts to caregivers.
  • End-to-end integration: hardware, firmware, and a cloud monitoring dashboard working as one system.
  • Robust error handling that kept uptime above 99%, because an alert system that goes down is worse than none.

Designed for people

The system was designed around the needs of two groups: the elderly users who should barely notice it, and the caregivers who need clear, timely alerts rather than noise.

To addAdd what you learned from the households or caregivers (interviews, feedback, a change you made because of it), plus photos of the hardware and a dashboard screenshot.

What it taught me

Reliability is a UX feature. For a safety product, graceful failure and trustworthy alerts matter more than any individual feature, and it's the reason I wanted to study design alongside engineering.
