SeniorGuard

Sensors in older folks' homes that alert caregivers when something might be wrong.

Role
Hardware, firmware, cloud
When
November 2024
Scale
10+ homes
Uptime
99%+

The problem

Someone living alone can be totally fine, until they're not.

Families want to know their parents or grandparents are okay. But nobody wants constant check-in calls or cameras everywhere.

And whatever you build has to work every time, without making anyone learn a new gadget.

What I built

  • A sensor network in 10+ homes, written in C/C++.
  • Real-time monitoring that alerts caregivers automatically.
  • Hardware, firmware, and a cloud dashboard that all work together.
  • A lot of error handling so it stays up 99%+ of the time. An alert system that goes down is worse than not having one.

Who it's for

I designed it for two groups. The older people living with it, who should barely notice it's there. And the caregivers, who need clear alerts, not a bunch of noise.

What I learned

For something like this, being reliable is the user experience.

That's a big reason I wanted to study design along with engineering.
