AirPods case redesign

A team redesign of the AirPods charging case: a hidden rear hinge, a snap latch, and an inertia lock that trips during a fall.

Try it

Open it, peek inside, drop it.

The viewer below is the project itself, running live. Open and close the lid, toggle see-through mode to find the spring, switch camera views, or run the drop test.

The redesign

  • Hidden rear hinge. The lid swings on a hinge tucked out of sight, so the case stays one clean form.
  • Snap latch with a hook. A hook catches and releases as the lid closes and opens.
  • A spring you can see. The viewer's see-through mode and spring close-up show how the mechanism stores and releases force.
  • Inertia lock. In free fall, the lock trips before impact so the lid stays shut when it matters most.

Why a viewer?

A mechanism is hard to explain in a static image. Building an interactive model meant teammates and instructors could understand the design by manipulating it, which is a lesson in communicating design through interaction.
