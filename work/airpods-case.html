AirPods case redesign

Our team redesigned the AirPods case with a hidden hinge, a snap latch, and a lock that kicks in when you drop it.

Try it

Open it, look inside, drop it.

This is the real model. Open and close the lid, turn on see-through mode to find the spring, switch camera angles, or run the drop test.

What we changed

  • Hidden rear hinge. The lid swings on a hinge you can't see, so the case looks like one clean piece.
  • Snap latch. A little hook catches when you close the lid and lets go when you open it.
  • A spring you can actually see. Flip on see-through mode or the spring close-up to watch it work.
  • Inertia lock. If the case starts falling, the lock trips before it hits the ground so the lid stays shut.

Why a 3D model

It's hard to explain how a hinge works with a picture. With a 3D model, you can just play with it and get it.

That's a big part of HCDE for me. Showing a design through how people interact with it.
