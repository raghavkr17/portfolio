The product

An assistant that remembers so you don't have to.

Muzings is a personal memory assistant built around two moments: capture, getting a thought, task, or detail in, and recall, getting it back when you need it. Both have to feel effortless, which makes them hard to test.

Visit muzings.ai

My role

I support product development and quality. The part I own is the testing process for new features: designing test cases, running them against the live product, and tracking recurring issues so engineering can fix root causes instead of one-off symptoms.

How I approach testing

  • Test the real thing. Cases run against the live product, the way a person would actually use it.
  • Test both halves. Capture and recall each get their own cases, and the interesting bugs often live in the handoff between them.
  • Track patterns, not just bugs. Recurring issues are logged so engineering can see what keeps coming back.
  • Write for people, not just pass/fail. The question behind each case is: would this feel right to a user?

Why it fits me

AI products are judged by trust. If someone captures a detail and recall gets it wrong, they stop relying on it. Testing like a user, and thinking like an engineer, is exactly the HCDE mix I'm building toward.
